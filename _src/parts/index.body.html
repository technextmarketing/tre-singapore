What is TRE®?

Your body already knows how to let go

TRE® is a series of seven gentle exercises that safely activate a natural reflex: a shaking or vibrating response called a neurogenic tremor. Developed by Dr. David Berceli, the method helps the body release deep muscular patterns of stress, tension and trauma — without needing to talk about what happened.

  • Simple to learn, and practised by hundreds of thousands of people worldwide
  • Self-regulated: you control the intensity and can stop at any time
  • Rooted in polyvagal theory and somatic neuroscience
  • Taught in Singapore by providers certified under the global TRE® programme
