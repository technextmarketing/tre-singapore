Preparation guide

Joining an online TRE™ session

Online sessions run on Zoom and work beautifully when the space around you is ready. A few minutes of preparation makes the difference.

  • Space: a quiet room where you can lie down with your whole body in view of the camera, ideally from the side.
  • Equipment: a yoga mat or carpet, a cushion for your head, a blanket, water, and a laptop or tablet (not a phone) placed at floor level.
  • Clothing: loose, comfortable clothes; bare feet or socks.
  • Timing: avoid a heavy meal in the two hours before, and keep 20–30 minutes free afterwards to rest.
  • Camera on: your provider watches for signs of over-activation and will guide you to slow down or stop.
