Preparing for your online TRE® session

Online sessions and group classes run on Zoom. Everything your facilitator can offer you comes through the camera, so a few minutes of set-up makes all the difference.

  • Camera: we must see you head to toe — standing and on the mat. Device about 2–2.5 m away, landscape, at hip height, to the side of your mat.
  • Kit: a yoga mat, wireless headphones (required in group classes), good lighting, loose light-coloured trousers, water and cushions within reach.
  • Safety: someone you trust nearby, with their name and phone number on your consent form.
  • Zoom: install the app, sign in with the email you registered with, test camera and microphone, and join through the app a few minutes early.
