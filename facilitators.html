Why it matters

What "certified" means

A Global TRE® Certified Provider has completed all three modules of the certification — personal practice, teaching one-to-one and teaching groups — including twelve online sessions of personal practice and supervision, under a Global TRE® Certifying Trainer accredited by TRE For All.

Certified providers are trained to recognise signs of over-activation, teach self-regulation, and adapt the exercises for different bodies and histories. That is what makes TRE® safe to learn.

Ask your provider

  • Which modules have you completed, and with which Certifying Trainer?
  • How do you help clients regulate if the tremor becomes intense?
  • Do you offer a first session that teaches me to practise safely at home?
  • What is your experience with my situation (pain, anxiety, burnout, performance)?
