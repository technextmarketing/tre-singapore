Whole site, TRE™ in Singapore (16 pages + JS-rendered event and facilitator pages). Mode: **Persuade** for home, about, education, facilitators, events, event, facilitator, contact. **Read** for the blog, the articles and the online-session guide. Rebuild layouts and heroes. ## Direction contract

THESIS: Release, made visible. Every page opens on a real TRE™ room seen through a living field of fine "tremor lines". They tremble where the pointer passes, then settle, so the site acts out charge → release → settle instead of describing it. It refuses the flat navy banner with decorative blobs, the stock wellness photo, and one fade-up repeated on every section.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy ink fields (#0f1f3d → #1c3d7a) carry the heroes and closes. Bronze and gold (#8b6b45 / #c9a45c) are the only drawn colour: hairlines, the tremor strokes, flow lines, node rings. Orange (#e08a2e) is reserved for the single primary action in view. Photography is real session rooms (wood floors, mats, garden light), duotoned toward navy as backgrounds and shown sharp inside rounded windows. Type is Plus Jakarta Sans 700 headings over Inter text. Icons are drawn SVG in one 1.75px stroke. There are no eyebrow labels, no stat-tile rows and no icon-card grids.

STORY: A newcomer sees that TRE™ is a natural body reflex, taught safely by certified people in Singapore. They watch it happen in real rooms and pick a provider or an event. A professional follows the Module 1 → 2 → 3 path with its real days, sessions, CCEUs and fees, then books the intake call. Each page ends on a real close offering both paths.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The inner-page hero is full-bleed at about 70vh on desktop and compact on phones. Behind everything, the page's photo is blurred and duotoned navy, densest on the left. The left 55% holds the existing H1, the lede, then the orange primary and an outline secondary. The right 40% is the same photo, sharp, in a 16px-radius window with a slow push-in and ±10px pointer parallax. Behind the text, a canvas of 7 tremor lines breathes on an 8s cycle; under the pointer they tremble and settle within ~1.2s. The home page keeps its full-screen video and gains the tremor field and a two-path chooser.

FORM: An extension of the incumbent TRE Singapore world (logo palette, technext.asia type), pinned by the user: "rebuild layouts, keep content, the way HummingBeing and BHD Asia were done". This is a precisely specified extension, so no concept-seed roll and no seed key.

Signature interaction: the tremor field (shake → settle). Motion grammar: continuous motion only in heroes, breath-paced. Sections get their own drawn motion (flows and timelines draw as they enter). Hovers run 180–260ms with exponential ease-out. Presses scale to .98, with no springs or bounces. Everything is visible by default and still under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
