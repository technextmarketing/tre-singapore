Who are we?

A community hub for TRE™ practitioners in Singapore

TRE™ in Singapore was established by Isabelle Claus Teixeira, a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer active in Singapore and worldwide. It exists to make TRE™ easy to find, safe to learn and rewarding to teach.

Our purpose

Release stress. Strengthen resilience. Improve wellbeing.

Singapore is one of the most demanding places in the world to live and work. TRE™ offers something rare here: a body-based practice that is simple, science-backed and self-empowering — you learn it once and keep it for life.

This platform brings together everyone who makes that possible:

  • Certified providers offering individual and group sessions, online and face-to-face
  • Trainees moving through the Global TRE™ Provider Certification
  • Allied professionals — coaches, therapists, HR leaders and movement teachers — integrating TRE™ into their work
  • First-timers who simply want to feel calmer, sleep better and recover faster
Who is Isabelle?

From Fortune 100 boardrooms to the mat

Isabelle Claus Teixeira spent more than 30 years as a Senior HR Business Partner and Talent Management leader in Fortune 100 companies across nine countries, collaborating with over 40 nationalities. Along the way she experienced first-hand what toxic work environments do to a body — and healed herself through somatic practice, TRE™ and mindfulness. That experience became her second career.

TRE™

Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer

Began practising TRE™ in 2017, became a Certified TRE™ Provider in 2019 and a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer in 2025 — accredited by the TRE™ For All organisation under Dr. David Berceli's methodology. Trained with David Berceli, Michelle Beck, Ching Meoh Cheong and Donna Philips.

Coaching

ICF PCC Executive Coach

ICF PCC-level certified executive coach since 2012 with more than 12 years of coaching experience across Southeast Asia, India, Singapore, Japan, the UK, the USA, Russia and Europe. Trained in Strozzi somatic bodywork and continuously studying transformational coaching, breathwork and trauma-informed techniques.

Mindfulness & energy work

Mindfulness Teacher & Reiki Healer

Trained as a Mindfulness Teacher in 2024 by Nick Cole, founder of the UK College of Mindful Meditation, and certified as an Accredited Usui/Holy Fire III Reiki healer in the William Lee Rand lineage.

A truly global practitioner

French by nationality, Isabelle is fluent in French, English and Spanish and conversant in Japanese and German. She has lived in ten countries — including ten years in Singapore — and is now a Japanese Permanent Resident based in Keihoku, north of Kyoto, travelling regularly to Singapore for certification modules, workshops and group sessions.

I don't do this work from a distance. I've walked through it myself.— Isabelle Claus Teixeira

How we work

Science-backed, body-first, people-centred
