TRE™ Module 1 ONLINE — with TRE Scotland (18–20 June 2027)

Friday 18 – Sunday 20 June 2027 · 18 Jun 14:00–17:00 · 19 & 20 Jun 09:30–18:00 (UK time) · Online · Zoom (the link is sent to registered participants) · Online · 2.5 days

With Isabelle Claus Teixeira & Vicki Cook (TRE Scotland)

ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1, online in collaboration with TRE Scotland. 2.5 training days in UK time, led by Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer Isabelle Claus Teixeira with Vicki Cook of TRE Scotland, in a small group of up to 15 participants, in English.

This is ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1 — the only TRE™ training anywhere in the world that carries ICF CCEUs (21 CCEUs: 16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies), issued on attendance and completion of the full 2.5 days.

TRE™ (Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises) was developed by Dr. David Berceli and activates the body's natural neurogenic tremoring mechanism — a reflex that releases stored stress and trauma, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation.

Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ for personal practice. With Modules 2 and 3 you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider. The group is limited to 15 participants and led by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer with an assisting provider.

Who it is for

  • A coach (in training or at ACC, PCC, MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • A therapist, psychologist or healthcare professional expanding a trauma-informed practice
  • A yoga or Pilates teacher, bodyworker or somatic practitioner
  • Anyone with chronic stress or burnout who is ready for a body-led approach
  • Those considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification

Programme

  • Day 1 · Fri 18 Jun 2:00–5:00 PM UK time · online — Self-Care & Presence for Coaches — An introductory afternoon on nervous system regulation and self-induced therapeutic tremoring, with TRE™ as a personal resilience and self-care practice.
  • Day 2 · Sat 19 Jun 9:30 AM–6:00 PM UK time · online — TRE™ Module 1 — Part 1 — The neuroscience of neurogenic tremoring, the TRE™ exercise sequence, somatic awareness, containment and grounding. Guided full TRE™ sessions with direct feedback from the trainers.
  • Day 3 · Sun 20 Jun 9:30 AM–6:00 PM UK time · online — TRE™ Module 1 — Part 2 & Completion — Deepen the practice, integrate the learning and complete Module 1, with Q&A, personal practice time and guidance toward Modules 2 and 3.

What is included

  • Module 1 Certification — valid as Module 1 of the Global TRE™ Provider Certification Program
  • 21 ICF CCEUs — 16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies
  • 2.5 training days — including the Day 1 afternoon workshop for coaches
  • Worldwide recognition under the Global TRE™ Certification Program
  • A small group of up to 15 participants
  • Not included: individual TRE™ sessions — add them at £85 per session

Fees

  • Normal price (after the early bird) — £589
  • Personal TRE™ session (optional add-on · not included) — £85 — per session

All prices in British pounds (GBP). Limited to 15 participants. Individual TRE™ sessions are not included and can be added at £85 each.
