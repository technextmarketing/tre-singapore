The Art of Conscious Rest — Experiencing deep relaxation through Yoga Nidra

Sunday 24 January 2027 · 8:00–10:00pm Singapore time (SGT) · Zoom (link shared with registered participants) · Live online

With Olga Titkova

Our increasingly busy and digitally connected lives expose us to constant stimulation and persistent demands on our attention. These conditions can contribute to accumulated physical and mental tension, making it difficult to experience genuine rest.

This experiential workshop introduces Yoga Nidra, a practice rooted in the Tantric yoga tradition that invites deep relaxation while cultivating awareness.

Beginning with gentle movement to prepare the body, participants will be guided through conscious breathing and systematic relaxation toward a state of deep rest, where the body relaxes, the mind becomes quiet, and awareness may remain present.

The practice will be followed by personal reflection and group discussion, allowing participants to share their experiences and insights.

Live on Zoom, Sunday 24 January 2027, 8:00–10:00pm Singapore time. The Zoom link is shared with registered participants before the session.

Who it is for

  • TRE™ practitioners (Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises) interested in exploring Yoga Nidra as a complementary approach to deep relaxation, body awareness, and conscious rest
  • Yoga & Mindfulness Practitioners — individuals seeking to deepen their experience of relaxation, cultivate awareness, and explore the traditional practice of Yoga Nidra
  • Corporate & Wellness Professionals — individuals navigating demanding work environments, sustained mental fatigue, or disrupted sleep who are interested in exploring conscious rest as a way to support relaxation and mental wellbeing
  • Anyone Seeking Deep Rest — individuals curious about experiencing profound relaxation and discovering a different approach to rest in everyday life
  • No previous Yoga Nidra experience is required

Programme

  • Sun 24 Jan 8:00–10:00pm SGT · Zoom — The Art of Conscious Rest — live online workshop — Gentle movement to prepare the body, conscious breathing, an extended guided Yoga Nidra practice, then personal reflection and group discussion.

What is included

  • Explore Awareness — discover the difference between thinking about the body and directly experiencing sensations through a simple guided exercise
  • Prepare for Deep Rest — explore conscious breathing and gentle physical preparation to support relaxation and stillness
  • Discover Yoga Nidra’s Roots — learn about the traditional foundations of Yoga Nidra and its effects in modern life
  • Experience Yoga Nidra — participate in an extended guided practice, moving through bodily sensations and breath toward deep relaxation and inner stillness
  • Reflect and Integrate — share observations and explore how conscious rest may support wellbeing in everyday life

Facilitators

  • Olga Titkova, Yoga & Mindfulness Teacher · E-RYT 200, RYT 500, YACEP. Olga has practiced yoga since 1997 and has been teaching since 2006. Her studies span traditional Hatha, Tantra, and Kundalini yoga, pranayama, meditation, and Yoga Nidra. She has also completed mindfulness teacher training in Oxford, UK. Alongside her yoga journey, Olga spent 27 years in the high-tech industry, leading global teams and technology operations across multiple countries. Her experience in demanding corporate environments has shaped her understanding of the importance of awareness, balance, and recovery in everyday life. Today, she brings together her yoga and mindfulness studies with her professional experience, integrating physical practice with awareness of bodily sensations, thoughts, and emotions to support personal wellbeing, inner balance, and a more conscious approach to everyday life.
